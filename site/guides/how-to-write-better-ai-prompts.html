How to Write Better AI Prompts (Beginner Guide)

Simple rules for getting clearer, more useful answers from any AI tool.

Why prompts matter

An AI tool can only work with the information you give it. A vague request such as 'write about solar panels' gives a generic answer. A clear request gives a useful one. The good news is that better prompts are easy to learn.

The 5 part prompt formula

  • Role: tell the AI who it should act as, for example 'You are an experienced travel blogger'.
  • Task: say exactly what you want, such as 'Write a 300 word introduction'.
  • Context: add details like your audience, country and goal.
  • Format: ask for bullet points, a table, or short paragraphs.
  • Limits: add word count, tone and things to avoid.

A before and after example

Weak prompt: Write about video editing laptops.

Better prompt: You are a tech reviewer. Write a 250 word buying guide for beginners who want a laptop for video editing in Premiere Pro. Use a friendly tone, include three key specs to check, and finish with a short checklist.

Improve the answer with follow-ups

You do not need a perfect first prompt. Treat it like a conversation. Ask the tool to shorten, simplify, add examples or change the tone. Each follow-up usually improves the result.

Always check the result

AI tools can make mistakes or sound sure about things that are wrong. Verify facts, numbers and names before you publish or rely on them.
