Perplexity

An AI search engine that answers questions and shows its sources.

Visit official website →

What is Perplexity?

Perplexity works like a mix of search engine and chatbot. It gives a written answer and links to the pages it used, which makes it easier to check facts. Great for research.

Key features

  • Answers with listed sources
  • Follow-up questions in the same thread
  • Good for quick research and comparisons
  • Focus modes for different kinds of searches

Who is it best for?

Students, bloggers and researchers who want sources next to the answer.

👍 Pros

  • Sources make fact-checking easier
  • Fast answers for research topics
  • Simple, clean interface

👎 Cons

  • Free plan limits some advanced features
  • Always open the sources for important claims

How to get started

  1. Open the Perplexity website
  2. Ask a research question, for example 'Compare solar panel types'
  3. Click the cited sources to verify the details
Plans, free limits and features can change at any time. Confirm current details on the official Perplexity website.
