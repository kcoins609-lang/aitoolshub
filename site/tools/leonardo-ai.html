What is Leonardo AI?

Leonardo AI is an image generator popular with designers and game artists. It offers different styles and models, plus tools to refine and edit images. Free accounts get a limited amount of credits.

Key features

  • Text to image with multiple styles
  • Image editing and refinement tools
  • Good for concept art and characters
  • Free credits for testing

Who is it best for?

Creators who need stylised artwork, thumbnails and concept visuals.

👍 Pros

  • Many styles to experiment with
  • Active community
  • Free credits to start

👎 Cons

  • Free credits are limited
  • Best results need prompt practice

How to get started

  1. Create a free account on the official site
  2. Write a detailed prompt: subject, style, lighting, mood
  3. Generate, pick your favourite, and refine with variations
Plans, free limits and features can change at any time. Confirm current details on the official Leonardo AI website.
