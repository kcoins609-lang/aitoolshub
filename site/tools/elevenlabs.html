What is ElevenLabs?

ElevenLabs converts text into natural sounding speech in many voices and languages. It is widely used for video narration, voice-overs and audio content. Free accounts get a monthly character allowance.

Key features

  • Text to speech with natural voices
  • Multiple languages
  • Voice customisation options
  • Free monthly allowance

Who is it best for?

YouTubers and video editors who need voice-overs without recording.

👍 Pros

  • Very natural sounding voices
  • Great for narration
  • Simple to use

👎 Cons

  • Free allowance is small
  • Only clone voices you have permission to use

How to get started

  1. Sign up on the ElevenLabs website
  2. Paste your script and choose a voice
  3. Generate the audio and download it for your video editor
Plans, free limits and features can change at any time. Confirm current details on the official ElevenLabs website.
