What is Descript?

Descript turns your recording into a transcript so you can edit the video by deleting or rearranging words. It also helps remove filler words and improve audio.

Key features

  • Edit video by editing text
  • Automatic transcription
  • Filler word removal
  • Screen recording

Who is it best for?

Podcasters, educators and YouTubers who talk to camera.

👍 Pros

  • Very fast for talking-head videos
  • Easy transcription
  • Beginner friendly editing style

👎 Cons

  • Free plan has limits
  • Not a replacement for advanced timeline editors

How to get started

  1. Create a Descript account
  2. Import your recording and let it transcribe
  3. Edit the text, then export the video
Plans, free limits and features can change at any time. Confirm current details on the official Descript website.
