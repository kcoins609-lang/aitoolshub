What is Suno?

Suno generates songs from a text prompt, including music and vocals. It is fun for background music ideas, jingles and creative experiments.

Key features

  • Text to song
  • Choose genre and mood
  • Lyrics you write or generate
  • Free daily credits

Who is it best for?

Creators wanting custom background music ideas.

👍 Pros

  • Fast and fun
  • No music skills needed
  • Many genres

👎 Cons

  • Free plan usage rules may limit commercial use, read the terms
  • Quality varies between generations

How to get started

  1. Open the Suno website and sign in
  2. Describe the style, for example 'calm acoustic background music'
  3. Generate, listen and keep the best version
Plans, free limits and features can change at any time. Confirm current details on the official Suno website.
