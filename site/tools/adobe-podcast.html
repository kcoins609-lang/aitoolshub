Adobe Podcast Enhance

Clean up noisy voice recordings so they sound like a studio mic.

Visit official website →

What is Adobe Podcast Enhance?

Adobe Podcast's Enhance Speech tool reduces background noise and improves voice clarity using AI. It is very useful for phone recordings and video voice-overs.

Key features

  • Noise reduction
  • Voice clarity improvement
  • Simple upload workflow
  • Free to try

Who is it best for?

Video editors and creators who record voice on a phone.

👍 Pros

  • Big improvement on noisy audio
  • Very easy
  • Fast results

👎 Cons

  • Free usage limits apply
  • Over-processed audio can sound artificial, so compare with the original

How to get started

  1. Open the Adobe Podcast website and sign in
  2. Upload your voice recording and apply Enhance
  3. Download and use it in your video project
Plans, free limits and features can change at any time. Confirm current details on the official Adobe Podcast Enhance website.
