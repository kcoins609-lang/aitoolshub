What is Pika?

Pika is an AI video generator for short clips. You can animate an image or describe a scene in text. It is a fun way to make stylised clips for social media.

Key features

  • Text to video
  • Image to video
  • Creative effects
  • Free credits to try

Who is it best for?

Social media creators who want quick animated clips.

👍 Pros

  • Simple interface
  • Fun effects
  • Quick results

👎 Cons

  • Short clip length
  • Free credits are limited

How to get started

  1. Create an account on the Pika website
  2. Upload an image or enter a prompt
  3. Generate, preview and download your clip
Plans, free limits and features can change at any time. Confirm current details on the official Pika website.
