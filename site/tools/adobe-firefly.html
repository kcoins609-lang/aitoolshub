What is Adobe Firefly?

Adobe Firefly is Adobe's family of generative AI tools. It generates images from text and offers features like generative fill that fit naturally with Photoshop and other Adobe apps.

Key features

  • Text to image
  • Generative fill and expand
  • Works alongside Adobe apps
  • Free starter credits

Who is it best for?

Designers and editors who already use Photoshop or Premiere Pro.

👍 Pros

  • Fits Adobe workflows
  • Polished and reliable interface
  • Designed with creators in mind

👎 Cons

  • Monthly credits are limited on free use
  • Some features need an Adobe subscription

How to get started

  1. Open the Firefly website and sign in with an Adobe ID
  2. Choose text to image or generative fill
  3. Download your result or send it to Photoshop
Plans, free limits and features can change at any time. Confirm current details on the official Adobe Firefly website.
