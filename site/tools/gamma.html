What is Gamma?

Gamma creates presentations and documents from a short prompt. You edit the generated slides and share or export them. A fast way to get a first draft of a deck.

Key features

  • Prompt to presentation
  • Editable themes and layouts
  • Share by link or export
  • Free starter credits

Who is it best for?

Students, freelancers and teams who need slides quickly.

👍 Pros

  • Very fast first draft
  • Modern looking themes
  • Easy editing

👎 Cons

  • Free credits are limited
  • Always rewrite generic text before presenting

How to get started

  1. Sign up on the Gamma website
  2. Enter your topic and choose a style
  3. Edit slides and present or export
Plans, free limits and features can change at any time. Confirm current details on the official Gamma website.
