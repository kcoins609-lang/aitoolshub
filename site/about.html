About Us

AIToolsHub is an independent website that helps beginners discover and understand free AI tools. AI tools appear every week, and it is hard to know which ones are worth your time. We collect them in one place and explain them in simple language.

What you will find here

  • A directory of AI tools sorted by what you want to do
  • A page for each tool with features, pros, cons and getting started steps
  • Beginner guides on prompts, choosing tools and using AI safely
  • Direct links to the official website of every tool

How we choose tools

We look for tools that have a free plan or free trial, a clear purpose, and an active official website. We explain what a tool does in our own words and we try to mention limits honestly.

Our promise

We aim to keep information accurate, but AI products change quickly. Prices, limits and features may be different by the time you visit, so please confirm details on the official site. If you notice something outdated, tell us and we will fix it.
